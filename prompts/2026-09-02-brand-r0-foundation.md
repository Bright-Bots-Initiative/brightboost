## Lessons

- Label secrets with the environment they belong to; a guard can enforce a label even when it cannot see the upstream project.
- Vite's `%VITE_X%` HTML replacement leaves the placeholder when the variable is absent — the smoke must treat a literal placeholder as "undeclared".
